Resources
What I'm using
🎥
3Blue1Brown — Essence of Linear Algebra + Essence of Calculus
Free on YouTube. Geometric intuition before grinding through textbook problems. Watch these first, then do Strang.
Starts: May 2026
📐
Strang — Introduction to Linear Algebra (ch1-8)
The canonical linear algebra reference. Imperial professors know it. Chapters 1-6 core, chapters 7-8 (SVD) before interview prep. Do the problem sets — passive reading is worthless.
Starts: Jun 2026
🎲
Harvard Stat 110 — Probability (Joe Blitzstein)
All 34 lectures, free on YouTube. Rigorous without being measure-theoretic — exactly the right level for MSc prerequisites. Covers MLE, Bayes, information theory foundations.
Starts: Sep 2026 (parallel with calculus)
📘
Mathematics for Machine Learning — Deisenroth, Faisal & Ong
Free PDF. Ties linear algebra, calculus, and probability together in ML notation. Ch5 (vector calculus) is a critical gap — covers partial derivatives, Jacobian, Hessian needed for backpropagation derivations. Use as capstone after Strang + Stat 110.
Ch5 (calculus): Aug 2026 · Ch6-7 (prob, optimisation): Apr 2027
📗
AIMA — Russell & Norvig, 4th Edition
Owned. The definitive AI textbook. Read in layers — chapters 1–2 now, ML chapters alongside Y2 T2, systematic cover-to-cover in Year 3 EPA period.
Chapters 1–2: now · Systematic read: Sep 2027
🎓
DeepLearning.AI Specialisations — Andrew Ng
ML Specialisation, Deep Learning Specialisation (5 courses), NLP or MLOps Specialisation. Internationally recognised credentials.
Starts: Oct 2026
🔥
fast.ai — Practical Deep Learning
Practical PyTorch course. Top-down approach — build things first, understand theory later. Complements Deisenroth's bottom-up approach.
Starts: Jan 2027
📐
MIT OCW 6.006 — Introduction to Algorithms
Free. Algorithms, data structures, computational complexity. Needed to strengthen the "substantial computing component" for Imperial.
Starts: Sep 2027
🏆
Kaggle
Real ML competitions with verifiable results. Target: 2 competitions, top 20–25% finish on at least one. Rankings visible to admissions tutors.
Starts: Oct 2026
